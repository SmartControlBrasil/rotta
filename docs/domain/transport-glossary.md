# Transport Domain Glossary

Initial glossary for Rotta. Terms are subject to refinement during discovery with the partner transport company.

- Organization: company or institutional participant in Rotta, such as carrier, customer, supplier, partner, or fleet owner.
- Carrier: organization or party responsible for providing transport capacity or executing transport services.
- Customer: organization or person requesting logistics services.
- Driver: person who executes or supports transport in the field. May be employee, aggregated partner, autonomous contractor, or third party.
- Vehicle: transport asset used to execute a service, such as motorcycle, car, van, or truck.
- Load: cargo or logistics need. A `Load` can exist before a driver, vehicle, or confirmed operation exists.
- Transport Request: customer request for a transport service, potentially generating quotes, loads, and later shipments.
- Quote: price and service proposal for a transport request or load.
- Shipment: operational execution of transport. A `Shipment` represents the real movement/service operation.
- Stop: pickup, delivery, return, or intermediate location in a shipment.
- Operational Event: relevant event in the transport lifecycle, such as assigned, arrived, collected, delayed, delivered, canceled, or failed.
- Proof of Delivery: evidence that delivery happened, such as signature, photo, document, canhoto, or digital confirmation.
- Settlement: calculation and record of amounts owed to drivers, partners, carriers, or other operational parties.
- Subscription: recurring commercial arrangement for customer access, platform usage, or contracted services.
